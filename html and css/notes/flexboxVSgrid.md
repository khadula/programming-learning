for grid:
- it is more rigid
- this is because you define the order of the columns and their sizes in the overall div container
- so if you move around your order of inner div elements, it is determined by the overall div

for flexbox:
- more flexibile
- since the size of the column (100px, flex: 1, flex: 2, etc) is determined in the inner div elements, you can move them around and keep them as the same size.
